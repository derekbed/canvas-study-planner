Coursewise local edition privacy

When you connect a school's HTTPS Canvas site, Coursewise requests Chrome access to that exact site. It reads your courses, enrollment grades, assignments, and announcements through your signed-in Canvas session. It uses them in your browser to show course cards, deadlines, and summaries.

Coursewise stores connected school domains, course names, appearance choices, course pictures, and numeric IDs of assignments you mark as read in Chrome local storage. The extension does not transmit this data to a Coursewise server or use Chrome sync storage. Canvas requests go only to the connected school's Canvas domain.

Coursewise does not sell data, display ads, or alter Canvas grades or submissions. Disconnect a school in the side panel to remove its saved course settings and revoke its site access. Removing the extension or clearing its storage deletes all local Coursewise data. Canvas retains its own data under your school's policies.

Use of information obtained through Chrome APIs is limited to Coursewise's Canvas appearance and study summary features. Coursewise does not transfer it to third parties for advertising or allow the developer to read it.

For privacy questions, use the developer support contact shown on the Chrome Web Store listing.

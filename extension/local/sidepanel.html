Coursewise

Before you use Coursewise

After you connect a school, Coursewise reads courses, grades, assignments, and announcements from your signed-in Canvas account to show appearance controls and study summaries. Coursewise saves appearance choices, course names and pictures, and IDs of items you mark as read in this browser. It does not send this data to a Coursewise server.

Coursewise does not change your Canvas submissions or grades. Read the privacy notice.

Connect your school's Canvas

Enter the HTTPS address of the Canvas site where you sign in. Chrome will ask for access to that site only.

Connected schools

    After connecting, reload your Canvas dashboard. Use its three-dot menu for Coursewise appearance.

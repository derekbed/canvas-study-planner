Before you use Coursewise on Canvas

Coursewise reads courses, grades, assignments, announcements, and visible page text from your signed-in Canvas account to show study tools. It stores appearance settings and read-item IDs in this browser. If you pair your Coursewise account, you can upload course files and save chats there. Page text and questions are sent to Coursewise and OpenAI only when you submit a question or request AI analysis.

Read the privacy notice before continuing.
